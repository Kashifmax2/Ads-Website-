About the desk

Last reviewed: 11 November 2026

TechSarthi is a small, independent technology desk. We publish short guides about the devices people already own — phones, chargers, laptops and home networks — and we try to answer one question per page: what should you actually do next?

How the guides are tested

Every step in a guide is carried out on hardware we own before it is published. If a fix needs a tool we do not have, we say so rather than describing it second-hand. Where a recommendation depends on your provider, your building or your phone model, the guide says that too.

We do not accept payment for a recommendation, and we do not put affiliate links inside the body of a guide. If that ever changes, the affected paragraph will carry a clear label above it.

How the site is funded

TechSarthi is reader-supported and funded by display advertising. Ads appear in clearly labelled spaces between articles, never inside a sentence and never disguised as editorial. Advertisers have no sight of a guide before publication and no influence over what it says.

We do not run pop-ups that block the page, we do not ask for notification permission on arrival, and we do not use ads that pretend to be a system warning.

Corrections

Technology changes, and so do menus, prices and model numbers. If a step no longer matches what you see on your device, tell us and we will check it. Corrected guides carry a new review date at the top, and substantive changes are described in the guide itself rather than quietly edited out.

What we will never do

  • Ask for an OTP, UPI PIN, bank password or card number.
  • Publish a guide that exists only to carry advertising.
  • Recommend a product because a brand paid for it.
  • Use fake countdowns, fake "last chance" warnings or invented reviews.

Contact

Corrections, privacy requests and anything else: hello@techsarthi.site. We read everything and reply to most messages within a few days.
