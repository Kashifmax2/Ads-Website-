5 easy ways to edit photos with AI

6 min read November 2, 2026

Photo editing used to need expensive software and hours of practice. Now free AI tools can remove a background, sharpen an old blurry picture, and make a phone portrait look like it came from a studio — all in a few minutes.

The easiest way to start is to upload your photo to a good browser-based tool and change only what you actually need. Stacking too many filters at once rarely looks natural; it usually makes the photo look plastic instead.

Five uses that come up again and again:

  • Removing backgrounds: perfect for product shots and profile pictures.
  • Sharpening photos: for old memories that came out a little soft.
  • Reducing noise: cleans up the grain in low-light photos.
  • Portrait retouching: small, gentle fixes to skin tone and light.
  • Upscaling: makes a small image look clean at up to 2x size.

One important note: free AI tools often add a watermark. Check the preview before you save, and never upload personal photos to a site whose privacy policy you don't understand.

Mobile · Buying guide

6 things that matter when buying a budget smartphone

5 min read October 30, 2026

Long spec sheets make it harder to pick the right phone, not easier. In reality, only four things matter for most people: the processor, the battery, the screen, and how long the software gets updates.

If your budget is around ₹15,000, last year's flagship or this year's mid-range phone is usually the best value. Wait a month after a new launch — prices tend to settle quickly.

Check this list before you buy:

  • At least a 5000mAh battery and 33W charging.
  • An AMOLED screen if you watch a lot of video.
  • At least two years of promised Android updates.
  • 8GB of RAM is enough for almost everything.
  • Make sure a service centre exists in your city.
  • Look at real camera samples in reviews, not just megapixels.

7 UPI mistakes to avoid when sending money

4 min read October 27, 2026

UPI is convenient, but one small careless moment can be expensive. The most common mistake is scanning a QR code or opening a link from an unknown number without checking it first. Remember: you never need your PIN just to receive money.

Never share an OTP or UPI PIN on a call about "cashback", a "lottery", or an "account closure". Your bank will never ask for your PIN over the phone.

Seven safety habits worth keeping:

  1. Check the recipient's name before every payment.
  2. Blur your UPI ID before sharing screenshots.
  3. Don't do banking on public Wi-Fi.
  4. Keep the app lock turned on in your UPI app.
  5. Don't enter your PIN for a "money request" — that's not a payment.
  6. Remove your UPI ID from any old phone number.
  7. If anything feels off, call your bank's helpline immediately.

8 simple ways to speed up your home Wi-Fi

5 min read October 24, 2026

Slow Wi-Fi is not always your ISP's fault. Router placement, old devices and background downloads are the three biggest culprits. Start by placing the router in the middle of your home, in the open, and a little above floor level.

If your router supports both 2.4GHz and 5GHz, use 5GHz for nearby devices and 2.4GHz for rooms farther away. Updating the router's firmware also improves speed and stability more often than people expect.

  • Keep the router away from microwaves and fridges.
  • Restart the router once a month.
  • Remove old devices from the network.
  • Use a strong Wi-Fi password and WPA3.
  • Pause background updates during video calls.
  • Add a mesh node if you have dead zones.
  • Run a speed test over Ethernet too.
  • If the router is very old, consider replacing it.
