Last updated: November 2026

Advertising and cookies

This site displays third-party advertisements. These ad networks may use their own cookies or web beacons so they can show you more relevant ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

You can turn off personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and manage other networks at AboutAds. Disabling cookies in your browser may affect some site features.

What we never do

  • We never ask for OTPs, UPI PINs, bank passwords or card numbers.

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information. Every newsletter email includes an unsubscribe link.
