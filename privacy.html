Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This page explains what TechSarthi collects when you read a guide, what the advertising partners on this site do with cookies, and how to switch personalised advertising off. It covers this website only.

What we collect

  • Page views. Which pages are read, how long a page stays open, the referring page, browser and device type, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. This is aggregated and used to decide which guides to write next.
  • Technical logs. Standard server logs kept by our hosting provider, including IP address and request time, for security and abuse prevention.
  • Nothing you do not give us. There is no account system and no newsletter signup on this site. If you email us, we keep your message and address only to reply.

Advertising and cookies

This site is funded by display advertising. Advertising partners may set their own cookies or read device information to limit how often you see an ad, measure whether it was viewed, and — where you have consented — show advertising based on your interests.

We do not pass your name, email address or any account information to advertisers, because we do not have it. The identifiers used for advertising are set by the advertising partner in your browser, not by us.

To turn off personalised advertising:

  • Google Ads Settings covers Google and its partners.
  • AboutAds and Your Online Choices cover many other networks.
  • Your browser's own cookie controls will block third-party cookies entirely. Some ads may still appear, but they will not be personalised.

Blocking cookies does not break any part of this site; there is no feature that depends on them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to certain processing. Because we hold almost nothing beyond server logs, most requests can be answered quickly.

To make a request, email hello@techsarthi.site with enough detail for us to find the relevant log entries. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is written for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

What we never do

  • We never ask for OTPs, UPI PINs, bank passwords or card numbers.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use ads that pretend to be a system warning or a security alert.
  • We do not open new tabs or windows without a click.

Changes to this policy

If the way this site handles data changes — for example if a newsletter is added — this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Substantive changes will be described here rather than made silently.
